Terms of Membership

Version 2026-11-09 · Last updated 9 November 2026

These terms apply when you apply to or become a member of the Galen Society of Medical Professionals (“GSMP”). By applying you agree to them. Questions: info@gsmpglobal.org.

1. Applying

Anyone aged 18+ studying or working in medicine or a related health field may apply. GSMP reviews each application and may approve or decline it at its discretion. Applying is free.

2. Fee & renewal

  • The annual membership fee (currently 100 USD) is payable only after approval. Membership becomes active when GSMP records your payment.
  • Membership runs for the period shown on your receipt. We remind you before it ends. If you do not renew, a 30-day grace period applies, after which your Verified Portfolio is paused (not deleted) until you renew.
  • Fees are non-refundable once membership is activated, except where the law requires otherwise. If GSMP cannot activate your membership after you pay, we refund you.

3. Verified Portfolio

  • “Verified” means GSMP confirmed that item with its issuer or an official source on the date shown. It is not a certification of clinical competence, licence to practise, or endorsement of any procedure.
  • “Self-declared” items are provided by you and not checked by GSMP.
  • You must provide accurate information and may only upload content you have the right to share. Patient-identifiable content requires the patient’s written consent and the hospital’s permission.
  • GSMP may correct, suspend or revoke a portfolio or item that is inaccurate or breaches these terms. A suspended or revoked portfolio shows that status to anyone who checks it.

4. Conduct

Members must act honestly and professionally, respect patient confidentiality, and not misuse GSMP’s name, logo or verification marks. GSMP may suspend or end a membership for serious or repeated breaches, including false credentials.

5. Your content

You keep ownership of what you upload. You allow GSMP to store and display it as needed to run the service, including on your public portfolio while it is active.

6. Liability

GSMP provides the service with reasonable care but cannot guarantee it will always be available or error-free. To the extent the law allows, GSMP is not liable for indirect losses, and its total liability to a member is limited to the fees that member paid in the previous 12 months.

7. Changes & law

We may update these terms and will notify members of significant changes. These terms are governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates.
