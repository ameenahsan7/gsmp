Galen Society of Medical Professionals

Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-09 · Last updated 9 November 2026

The Galen Society of Medical Professionals (“GSMP”, “we”) runs gsmpglobal.org, a network for medical students and doctors. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Contact us at info@gsmpglobal.org.

1. What we collect

  • Account & application: name, email, password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider), phone, gender, country, city, institution, degree, year, field, specialty, interests, referral code and your application statement.
  • Membership & payments: membership status and dates; payment amount, method, date and reference. We do not receive or store card numbers.
  • Verified Portfolio (if you create one): your story, photo, credentials, supporting links, photos you upload, and contact details of issuers you nominate to confirm a credential.
  • Activity: actions in the member and admin areas (for example events created or records changed), kept for security and audit.

2. Why we use it

  • To review applications and run your membership (contract with you).
  • To verify portfolio credentials, including contacting the issuer you nominate (with your request).
  • To send service emails: application decisions, receipts, renewal reminders, portfolio updates (contract / legitimate interest).
  • To keep financial records and meet legal obligations.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent fraud (legitimate interest).

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. What is public

Only what you choose to publish. Your Verified Portfolio page and member card are public once active and show your name, title, chapter and the items you publish; email and phone appear only if you switch them on. Evidence links, issuer emails and payment data are never public.

4. Patient privacy

You must not upload anything that identifies a patient — faces, names, wristbands, charts or screens showing patient data — unless the patient has given written consent and the hospital permits publication. Operating-theatre photos are reviewed by GSMP before they are published, and we remove any that do not meet this rule.

5. Who processes your data

  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage (hosted in the Asia-Pacific, Mumbai region).
  • Resend — sending our emails.
  • GitHub Pages — hosting the website (no member data is stored there).
  • GSMP administrators and, for your own chapter, your Chapter Lead.

These providers may process data outside your country. We use providers that apply appropriate safeguards.

6. How long we keep it

  • Membership and portfolio data: while your membership is active and up to 2 years after it ends, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Payment records: as long as required for accounting and tax purposes.
  • Rejected applications: up to 12 months.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, object to certain uses, or withdraw consent, by emailing info@gsmpglobal.org. Many details you can edit yourself in your dashboard. We reply within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

8. Security

Access is restricted by role at the database level, connections are encrypted, and administrative actions are logged. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at once if you suspect misuse of your account.

9. Age

GSMP membership is for people aged 18 or over.

10. Changes

We will post updates here and tell members by email about significant changes.
